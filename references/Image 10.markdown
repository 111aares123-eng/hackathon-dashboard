## Brand & Style

This design system combines a high-energy Pop-Art Y2K aesthetic with neo-brutalist digital mechanics, engineered specifically for the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Rajalakshmi Engineering College (REC). It breaks away from institutional academic monotony, replacing sterile enterprise dashboards with dynamic zine-style compositions, playful sticker collages, bold black ink outlines, and offset shadows. 

The target audience includes undergraduate engineers, competitive student hackers, AI researchers, and hackathon organizers who thrive in high-tempo, innovative environments. The interface evokes punchy optimism, relentless curiosity, and cultural vibrancy while maintaining the functional rigor and structural clarity required for academic schedules, project submissions, and hackathon registrations.

## Colors

The palette balances warm editorial paper tones against high-voltage pop primaries and acid accents drawn from retro print and screen culture:

- **Main Surface Canvas (`#FFF9E9`)**: Vintage warm cream that provides a tactile, printed-paper backdrop, eliminating digital eye strain.
- **Primary Accent (`#F12D2D`)**: Saturated pop red for primary calls to action, urgent hackathon countdowns, and high-impact badges.
- **Secondary Accent (`#1748F5`)**: Electric cobalt blue representing computational intelligence, used for interactive links, verified tags, and team headers.
- **Highlight (`#FFE23B`)**: Sunshine yellow applied to starburst highlights, prize callouts, and hover states.
- **Playful Accent (`#D9FF38`)**: Acid yellow-green for active status chips, hackathon "open" registrations, and high-energy alert banners.
- **Soft Accent (`#F4A7DB`)**: Bubblegum pink used in sticker decorations, secondary tags, and creative workshop categorizations.
- **Deep Ink (`#171717`)**: Dense carbon ink utilized for crisp borders, typography, and hard offset drop shadows.
- **Warm Paper (`#E9E2D5`)**: Card base tone, secondary tabs, and muted metadata containers.

Color accessibility must maintain an uncompromising contrast ratio: all functional text appears in `#171717` against surfaces, or in `#FFF9E9` when contained within saturated solid blocks like `#1748F5` and `#F12D2D`.

## Typography

Typography plays a structural and expressive role:
- **Display Typography (`Bricolage Grotesque`)**: Set in heavy weights with tight tracking, frequently styled in uppercase for punchy headlines, marquee announcements, hero slogans, and retro magazine flair.
- **Subheadings & Technical Architecture (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides geometric precision for section titles, dates, hackathon track identifiers, prize figures, and data visualizations.
- **Body & Longform Information (`DM Sans`)**: Handles reading comfort, team bios, registration guidelines, and academic disclosures with high optical legibility.

Display levels can adopt inline contrasting badges, uppercase text badges, or subtle 1-2° rotational tilts to recreate an authentic physical zine spread.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids soft blurred shadows, relying strictly on hard neo-brutalist offset drop shadows and clean black strokes:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Zero elevation with a standard `2px solid #171717` border.
- **Level 1 (Interactive Base / Badges)**: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #171717` with `2px solid #171717` borders.
- **Level 2 (Cards / Modals / Feature Tiles)**: `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #171717` with `2.5px solid #171717` borders.
- **Level 3 (Hover States / Floating Stickers)**: `box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #171717`, translating `-2px, -2px` on hover to evoke a physical peel-off effect.
- **Active / Pressed State**: `box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #171717`, translating `+4px, +4px` down and right, giving elements a mechanical, clicky tactile response.

## Shapes

The design uses tight, controlled corners (0.25rem to 0.5rem base radius) to maintain structural punch without becoming entirely sharp or overly rounded. 

Complementary geometric cutouts include:
- **Pill Tags**: `rounded-full` reserved for status chips and pill badges.
- **Starburst Badges**: Multi-point SVG shapes for "HACKATHON LIVE", "PRIZES", and "APPLY NOW".
- **Cutout Corner Frames**: 45-degree chamfered upper-right tabs on opportunity cards reminiscent of tech event entry tickets.
- **Rotational Play**: Interactive cards and stickers feature intentional tilt variants (`rotate(-1deg)` to `rotate(2deg)`) that straighten out on user focus.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#F12D2D` background, `2.5px solid #171717`, text in uppercase `Space Grotesk` (`#FFF9E9`), with a `4px 4px 0px #171717` drop shadow. On hover: shifts `-2px, -2px` with a `6px 6px 0px #171717` shadow. Active: snaps to `0px 0px 0px` shadow at `translate(4px, 4px)`.
- **Secondary Action**: `#1748F5` background with matching offset drop shadow and white text.
- **Tertiary / Neutral Action**: `#FFF9E9` background with `#171717` text and border.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status Indicator**: Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`), `2px solid #171717` border, `2px 2px 0px #171717` shadow. 
- Active tracks feature `#D9FF38` (Acid Green), deadline warnings use `#FFE23B` (Yellow), and department flags sit in `#F4A7DB` (Bubblegum Pink).

### Cards & Opportunity Frames
- Outer surface in `#FFF9E9` or `#E9E2D5` encased in a `2.5px solid #171717` border with a `6px 6px 0px #171717` shadow.
- Header bars feature contrasting solid backgrounds (e.g., `#1748F5` or `#F12D2D`) containing uppercase event titles, date chips, and starburst accents.

### Form Inputs
- Background in `#FFFFFF` with a `2px solid #171717` outline and a `3px 3px 0px #171717` shadow. Focus states swap the shadow color to `#1748F5` or `#F12D2D` with an internal focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Hard-edged square boxes (`2px roundedness`) with `2px solid #171717` borders. Checked state fills the box with `#F12D2D` or `#1748F5` featuring a crisp black checkmark icon.

### Sticker & Marquee Accents
- Infinite marquee banners running across viewports with `#FFE23B` or `#D9FF38` backgrounds, bordered top and bottom by `2px solid #171717`, displaying upcoming deadlines and hackathon tracks in bold uppercase text.
